Fair Play & Match Rules

Follow these rules to keep ArenaX fair for everyone.

Last Updated: 2026

1 Room ID & Password Timeline

Room credentials are published at fixed timings before every match. Join the custom room on time to avoid disqualification:

T-30 minutes
Match Reminder
A notification is sent to all joined players. Be ready and logged into BGMI / Free Fire.
T-15 minutes
Room Credentials Released
Room ID and Password become visible in the "My Matches" section and match details modal.
T-5 minutes
Final Call
Join the custom room immediately. Late entries may not be allowed once the host locks the room.
T-0 minutes
Match Starts
Room is locked. Late joins will result in entry fee forfeiture — no refunds.
⚠️
Do not share room credentials with anyone outside the match. This leads to permanent banning of all involved accounts.

2 Screenshot Proof Requirement

To claim any prize, kill reward, or dispute a result, you must provide valid screenshot proof.

  • Screenshot after every match: Capture the final results screen showing your kills, rank, and match summary.
  • Full-screen shots only: Cropped or edited images will be rejected.
  • Timestamp visible: The in-game clock must be visible in the screenshot.
  • Send within 15 minutes: Upload proof to the ArenaX support channel right after the match ends.
  • Video proof (if requested): Admins may request a screen recording for suspicious plays.
📸
Keep all screenshots for 7 days after a match in case a dispute is raised.
🚫
No proof = No prize. Claims submitted without valid proof will be rejected without exception.

4 Teaming = Immediate Disqualification

Teaming means cooperating with an opponent inside a solo, duo, or squad match to gain an unfair advantage. This includes:

  • Not shooting a nearby enemy on purpose.
  • Sharing loot, ammo, or vehicles with an opponent.
  • Reviving a player from an enemy team.
  • Coordinating positions or rotations with a rival squad.
⚠️
All players involved in teaming will be banned permanently, regardless of whether they started it or joined in.

5 Match Conduct Rules

  • IGN must match the one you registered with — random name changes = disqualification.
  • No smurfing: Using a low-level alt account in beginner lobbies = ban.
  • Follow admin instructions at all times during the match.
  • No streaming with delay under 3 minutes during active matches (stream-sniping risk).
  • Report suspicious players with proof — do not take matters into your own hands.

6 Reporting a Cheater

To report someone breaking the rules, send the following to support@arenax.gg:

  • Match title & match ID
  • Your IGN & Game UID
  • Screenshot or video proof
  • Brief description of what happened
✅
Verified reports may be rewarded with bonus balance as a thank-you for keeping ArenaX clean.
