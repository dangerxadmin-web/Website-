Refund & Wallet Policy

Everything you need to know about refunds, deposits, and withdrawals.

Last Updated: 2026

1 Refunds for Cancelled Matches

If a match is cancelled by ArenaX admins for any reason (not enough players, technical issues, server downtime, etc.), the following refund rules apply:

  • 100% of entry fee refunded to your wallet balance.
  • Refund is processed automatically within 24 hours.
  • Bonus balance used for the entry fee is restored to bonus balance.
  • Real balance used for the entry fee is restored to real balance.
  • You will receive a notification once the refund is processed.
✅
Auto-refund: No need to raise a ticket — cancelled match refunds are automatic.

2 Non-Refundable Entry Fees

Once you join a match, the entry fee is non-refundable. You will NOT receive a refund if:

  • You change your mind after joining.
  • You miss the match because you forgot the time.
  • You lose internet connection during the match.
  • Your device crashes, dies, or has technical issues.
  • You get banned for cheating or rule violations.
  • Your game account gets temporarily suspended by the publisher.
  • You arrive late to the custom room.
⚠️
Careful before you click JOIN: Entry fees are deducted immediately and are not reversible.

4 Withdrawal Process & Timeline

Withdrawals go through the following steps:

  1. Eligibility Check: You must have played at least 3 matches on ArenaX.
  2. Withdrawal Request: Enter your UPI ID and amount in the Wallet section.
  3. Admin Verification: Our team verifies your account and request.
  4. Payment Processing: Money is transferred to your UPI ID.
  5. Confirmation: You receive a notification once the transfer is complete.

5 Wallet Balance Types

ArenaX uses two separate balances:

  • Real Balance: Deposits + winnings. Fully withdrawable after the 3-match rule.
  • Bonus Balance: Sign-up bonus + first-deposit bonus + referral rewards. Only usable for tournament entries — never withdrawable.
💡
When you join a match, bonus balance is used first, then real balance.

6 Failed or Rejected Withdrawals

A withdrawal may be rejected or delayed if:

  • The UPI ID provided is invalid or inactive.
  • Suspicious activity is detected on the account.
  • The 3-match rule has not been fulfilled.
  • Your account has pending rule violations.
  • KYC verification (if requested) is incomplete.
✅
If a withdrawal is rejected, the amount is refunded to your wallet balance immediately.
