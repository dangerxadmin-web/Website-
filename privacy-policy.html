Privacy Policy

How ArenaX collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: 2026

1 Information We Collect

To operate the ArenaX tournament platform, we collect a limited set of information necessary for your account and payouts:

Data TypePurpose
In-Game Name (IGN)Match registration & participant identification
Game UIDLinking you to your in-game character for prize verification
Email AddressLogin, password reset, prize notifications

ℹ️
We do not collect your bank account, card number, or UPI PIN. All payment processing happens on secure third-party gateways.

2 How We Secure Your Data

Your data is protected with industry-standard security practices:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication uses HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Data stored on Firebase Firestore is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Authentication: Passwords are hashed with Firebase Auth and never stored in plain text.
  • Role-Based Access: Only authenticated admins can access sensitive user data.
  • Firestore Security Rules: Strict rules ensure users can only read/write their own data.
  • Regular Audits: We monitor for unauthorized access attempts.
🔐
Security First: We treat your data like it's our own — protected, private, and never sold.

3 No Third-Party Data Sharing

ArenaX does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

The only exceptions are:

  • Payment Processors: PayPal and similar gateways receive only the minimum data required to process your transaction.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law enforcement or a court order.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Firebase/Google Cloud) under strict data-processing agreements.
🚫
We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to game publishers, not to anyone.

4 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Transaction records are kept for up to 3 years for accounting and tax compliance.
  • Deleted accounts have their personal data removed within 30 days (except legally required records).

5 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your IGN, UID, or email from the Profile page.
  • Deletion: Request account deletion via support.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may close your account at any time.

6 Cookies & Local Storage

ArenaX uses minimal local storage and cookies only for:

  • Keeping you logged in (Firebase Auth session).
  • Remembering your game/mode filter preferences.
  • Improving load performance.

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

7 Children's Privacy

ArenaX is strictly for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a minor has registered, the account will be deleted immediately.
